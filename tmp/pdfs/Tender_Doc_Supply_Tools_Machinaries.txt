1. Introduction and Project Background

1.1 About the Procuring Entity
The North East Cane and Bamboo Development Council (NECBDC) is the nodal institution for the
promotion and development of the cane and bamboo sector in the North Eastern Region (NER) of India,
functioning under the aegis of the North Eastern Council (NEC), Ministry of Development of North Eastern
Region (DoNER), Government of India.

1.2 The Project
This procurement is undertaken under the flagship project titled “Strengthening of Traditional Bamboo
Artisan Clusters by Enhancing Market Access, Modernizing Products, and Linking with Digital and
Retail Ecosystems”, sanctioned for funding under the North Eastern Council (NEC). The project seeks to
transform the bamboo-based artisan economy of the NER - a region that holds over 60% of India’s bamboo
reserves - by blending traditional craft with modern technology, infrastructure and enterprise-building tools.
The intervention provides for the integrated development of 15 bamboo artisan clusters across all eight
states of the North East, benefitting 4,391 artisans. Each cluster is to be equipped with an upgraded Common
Facility Centre (CFC) functioning as a decentralised production unit, networked with a central Finishing &
Fulfilment Hub at NECBDC, Guwahati under a hub-and-spoke operational model.
Under the current intervention, each Common Facility Centre (CFC) to be developed or upgraded across
the identified bamboo artisan clusters will comprise two core structures: a work-shed for production,
training and finishing activities, and a warehouse/godown for raw material storage and inventory
management. Each unit will follow a standard floor plan of 15 ft × 25 ft, designed to meet key functional
and operational requirements while ensuring cost-efficiency, ease of replication and suitability across
cluster locations.

1.3 Purpose and Need for this Procurement
A core component of the project is the augmentation of CFC infrastructure through the procurement and
installation of modern, semi-mechanised tools, equipment and machineries - such as cutting, drilling,
sanding, planing, grinding and finishing tools, along with specialised machines for bamboo processing.
Alongside this, artisans at the CFCs are to be equipped with modern, durable hand tools issued as
standardised individual Tool Kit Boxes, covering cutting, drilling, chiselling, filing, shaping and finishing
operations for bamboo processing. Together, these assets enable collective access to advanced production
capability, ensure consistent workmanship across the cluster, improve productivity and product quality,
reduce production costs, and prepare artisan clusters for organised retail, e-commerce and export markets.
The supplied items are to be deployed at the CFCs of the 15 clusters as per the deployment plan (refer para
2.3) finalised by NECBDC. As per the project work-plan, procurement, delivery, installation, testing and
commissioning of tools, equipment and machineries, together with the supply and delivery of hand tools
and toolkit boxes, for the CFCs is scheduled in the infrastructure-setup phase (Year 1); bidders are
therefore expected to commit to time-bound delivery and commissioning.

2. Scope of Supply and Delivery Locations

2.1 Scope of Supply
The scope of supply under this tender comprises the supply, delivery to site, installation, testing,
commissioning, demonstration and comprehensive warranty support for the tools, equipment and
machineries listed at Part A, together with the supply and delivery of the hand tools and toolkit boxes listed
at Part B, of the Schedule of Requirements (Section 7). The scope specifically includes:
  1. Supply of brand-new, unused, latest-model goods conforming to (or exceeding) the technical
     specifications in Section 7, complete with all standard accessories required for operation.
  2. Safe transportation, insurance in transit, loading/unloading and delivery to the designated CFC
     location(s) across the 8 NER states.
  3. Installation, testing and commissioning of all machinery/equipment requiring installation, including
     any minor civil/electrical interfacing at site.
  4. On-site demonstration and basic operational/safety training for CFC personnel.
  5. Comprehensive on-site warranty and after-sales service for one (1) year from the date of successful
     delivery, installation, testing and commissioning.
  6. Assembly of each Tool Kit Box under Part B, complete with the full set of specified hand tools and
     accessories, securely packed, labelled and marked cluster-wise as per the Supply Order, together with
     warranty against manufacturing and material defects for one (1) year from the date of delivery and
     acceptance, including free replacement of defective items at the delivery site.

2.2 Rate-Contract Nature of the Procurement
This is a rate contract. Bidders shall quote firm unit rates for each item. The quantities indicated in the
Schedule of Requirements are indicative requirements reflecting the consolidated needs of the project.
NECBDC reserves the right to:
   • place orders item-wise and in such quantities, in one or more lots, as may actually be required during
      the contract validity;
   • vary (increase or decrease) the indicated quantities, and to deliver items to any of the 15 cluster CFC
      locations across the eight NER states;
   • issue Purchase/Supply Orders against the contracted unit rates throughout the contract validity period
      of one (1) year from the date of award, extendable by NECBDC.
The quoted unit rates shall remain firm and fixed for the entire contract period and shall not be subject to
any escalation on any account.

4. Instructions to Bidders (ITB)

4.1 Single Stage, Two Cover System
The tender follows a Single Stage, Two Cover System through online mode only, on the Central Public
Procurement Portal (CPP Portal) at https://eprocure.gov.in/eprocure/app. Bids shall be submitted in two
separate online covers:
    •   Cover I – Technical Bid: Scanned copies (in PDF) of all eligibility documents, declarations and
        applicable annexures (Annexures A to E), physically signed, without any price information.
    • Cover II – Financial Bid: Price Bid strictly in the BoQ format provided on the CPP Portal
        (Annexure F), to be filled and uploaded as prescribed.
Disclosure of any financial information in Cover I shall render the bid liable to rejection. Bids submitted in
physical form, by post, courier, fax or email shall not be entertained.

4.2 Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)
    •   The bidder shall submit an Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of Rs. 15,00,000.00/- by way of Demand
        Draft drawn in favour of North East Cane and Bamboo Development Council (NECBDC) payable
        at Guwahati. The EMD should remain valid for a period of 45 days beyond the final bid validity
        period.
    •   For the EMD furnished as Demand Draft (DD), a scanned copy shall be uploaded with the
        Technical Bid (Cover I), and the original instrument shall reach the office of the Managing Director,
        NECBDC, 13th Mile, G.S. Road, Burnihat, Guwahati – 793101, before the date and time of
        opening of Technical Bids, failing which the bid shall be rejected.
    •   MSEs registered on the Udyam Portal and DPIIT-recognised Start-ups are exempted from EMD,
        subject to uploading of valid registration certificates.
    •   The EMD of unsuccessful bidders shall be returned on or before the 30th day after award of the
        contract. However, the EMD of unsuccessful bidders during first stage, i.e. technical evaluation
        shall be returned within 30 days of declaration of result of first stage, i.e. technical evaluation. The
        EMD shall be returned/refunded without interest.
    •   The EMD of the successful bidder shall be returned after submission and acceptance of the
        Performance Security Deposit.
    •   EMD shall be forfeited if the bidder withdraws or modifies its bid during the validity period, or
        fails to furnish Performance Security Deposit or execute the agreement after award.

4.3 Preparation and Online Submission of Bids

5. Evaluation and Award

5.1 Technical Evaluation
    • Technical Bids (Cover I) shall be opened online on the CPP Portal on the scheduled date and time
       and scrutinised for completeness, responsiveness and fulfilment of the eligibility criteria specified
       in Section 3.
    •   NECBDC may seek clarifications on submitted documents; however, no new document materially

    •   Financial Bids (Cover II) of only those bidders found technically qualified shall be opened online,
        on a date intimated through the CPP Portal.
5.2 Financial Evaluation and Selection (L1)
    • The Financial Bid shall be submitted online under Cover II strictly in the BoQ format provided on
        the CPP Portal (Annexure F), quoting unit rates for each line item on FOR destination basis (CFC
        sites in the eight North Eastern States), inclusive of all costs, with GST indicated separately.
    • Bids shall be evaluated on the basis of the combined Grand Total of all items under Part A and Part
        B of the Schedule of Requirements taken together, calculated by multiplying the quoted unit rate
        by the indicated quantity for each item and summing the total across all items of both Parts,
        inclusive of GST. Partial bids covering only one Part shall be treated as non-responsive. The
        technically qualified bidder quoting the lowest such Grand Total shall be declared L1.
    • The Rate Contract shall be awarded to the L1 bidder. The unit rates quoted by the L1 bidder shall
        remain firm and fixed for the entire currency of the Rate Contract, including any extension.
    • Although the bidder shall be selected on L1 basis, issuance of the Work Order / Supply Order shall
        be made only after successful inspection and verification of the quality, specifications and
        suitability of the tools, equipment and machineries and the hand tools and toolkit boxes offered by
        the L1 bidder. NECBDC or its authorised representative(s) may inspect samples, technical
        literature, make/model details, or any other evidence considered necessary before issuance of the
        Work Order / Supply Order.
    • In case two or more bidders quote the same L1 (Lowest Evaluated Price), the bidder having the
        higher average annual turnover during the prescribed financial years shall be ranked higher. If the
        tie still persists, preference shall be given to the bidder having the higher cumulative value of
        similar work orders successfully executed. In the event of a further tie, the Evaluation Committee
        shall determine the successful bidder based on overall merit and suitability, and its decision shall
        be final and binding on all bidders.

    •   NECBDC shall issue a Letter of Acceptance (LoA) / Award of Rate Contract to the successful L1

        unconditional acceptance within seven (7) days.
    •   The successful bidder shall furnish Performance Security Deposit equal to 5% (five percent) of the

        fourteen (14) days of issue of LoA. The Performance Security shall remain valid up to the end of
        the one-year warranty period plus a claim period of sixty (60) days.
    •   Failure to furnish Performance Security Deposit or execute the agreement within the stipulated time
        shall entail forfeiture of EMD and cancellation of award.
    •   The Work Order / Supply Order shall be issued only upon successful inspection of the quality,
        specifications and suitability of the tools, equipment and machineries and the hand tools and toolkit

    • Supplies shall be made only against written Supply Orders issued by NECBDC during the currency

        the designated CFC sites within the period specified in each Supply Order / Work Order. Supply,
        delivery, installation, testing and commissioning of tools, equipment and machineries, and the
        supply and delivery of hand tools and toolkit boxes, shall commence within fifteen (15) days from
        the date of issuance of the Supply Order / Work Order and shall be completed within thirty (30)
        days from the date of receipt of the Supply Order / Work Order. The work shall be undertaken on
        a cluster-wise basis, with priority given to clusters where CFC sheds are ready for installation. The
        successful bidder shall not wait for all fifteen (15) clusters to complete construction before
        commencing supply and installation, but shall execute the work progressively as each cluster
        becomes ready. Transportation, loading/unloading and transit insurance shall be the sole
        responsibility of the supplier.
6.2 Inspection and Acceptance
    • Tools, equipment and machineries, and hand tools and toolkit boxes, shall be inspected on receipt
        at site by NECBDC or its authorised representative(s). Items found defective, damaged, sub-
        standard or not conforming to specifications shall be rejected and shall be replaced by the supplier
        free of cost within seven (7) days of intimation.
    • Acceptance of stores at site shall be evidenced by a Goods Receipt / Inspection Note signed by the
        consignee.
6.3 Warranty
All items shall carry a comprehensive on-site warranty of one (1) year from the date of successful delivery,
installation, testing and commissioning. During the warranty period, defective items shall be repaired or
replaced free of cost. Hand tools and toolkit boxes supplied under Part B shall carry a warranty of one (1)
year against manufacturing and material defects, reckoned from the date of successful delivery and
acceptance at site; items found defective during this period shall be repaired or replaced free of cost,
including freight and handling charges, within fifteen (15) days of intimation.
6.4 Payment Terms
    • For processing payments, the vendor shall submit pre-receipted GST invoice (in triplicate), delivery
       challans, consignee’s Goods Receipt / Inspection Note and warranty documents.
    • Payments shall be made through electronic transfer (PFMS) only, subject to deduction of statutory
       taxes (TDS/GST-TDS) as applicable.
    •   Final payments shall be made only after successful delivery, installation, testing and
        commissioning of the tools, equipment and machineries under Part A, and after successful delivery
        and acceptance of the hand tools and toolkit boxes under Part B, subject to successful verification
        / inspection report by NECBDC or its authorised representative.

6.5 Performance Security
The successful bidder shall furnish a Performance Security Deposit equal to 5% of the contract value by
Bank Gurantee / Demand Draft. The Performance Security shall remain valid up to the end of the one-year
warranty period also after successful delivery, installation and commissioning of machinery & tools with a
claim period of sixty (60) days. Failure to furnish Performance Security Deposit within the stipulated period
shall result in forfeiture of EMD and cancellation of award.

6.6 Liquidated Damages (LD)
In case of delay in delivery beyond the stipulated period attributable to the supplier, liquidated damages at
the rate of 0.5% (half percent) of the value of the delayed supplies per week of delay or part thereof, subject
to a maximum of 10% of the value of the delayed supplies, shall be levied, without prejudice to NECBDC’s
right to cancel the Supply Order and effect risk purchase at the supplier’s cost.
6.7 Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations if such failure results from events beyond
its reasonable control, including natural calamities, epidemics, war, civil unrest or Government actions,
provided the affected party notifies the other within fourteen (14) days and takes reasonable steps to mitigate
