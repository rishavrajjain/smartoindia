                                  Notice Inviting Tender (NIT)

The North East Cane and Bamboo Development Council (NECBDC) invites sealed competitive bids
under a Single Stage, Two Cover System (Technical Bid and Financial Bid) from eligible, reputed
and financially sound manufacturers / authorised dealers / suppliers for the supply, delivery,
installation, testing and commissioning of tools, equipment, machineries, hand tools and toolkit
boxes, on a rate-contract basis, for the up-gradation of Common Facility Centres (CFCs) at fifteen
(15) bamboo artisan clusters located across the eight states of the North Eastern Region (NER).
The salient details of this tender are summarised below. In case of any discrepancy between this
summary and the detailed clauses of the tender document, the detailed clauses shall prevail.
