     DAY 1
     Shimla To Sangla | Kinnaur Gate (Distance: Approx. 220 Km)
       Arrive at the designated boarding point in Delhi by evening and meet our representative.
       Arrive in Shimla, freshen up, and begin your journey towards Sangla.
       The drive takes you through the charming hill towns of Kufri and Narkanda along the historic
       Hindustan-Tibet Highway.
       Then, stop at the iconic Kinnaur Gate, the rock-cut entrance that welcomes you into the
       Kinnaur Valley.
       By evening, arrive in Sangla, check in, and relax after the long drive.
       You can explore the local Sangla Market, known for Kinnauri handicrafts, woollens, dry fruits,
       and small cafés.
       Dinner and overnight stay in Sangla.




     DAY 2
     Sangla To Nako Via Khab Sangam | Chitkul Excursion (Distance:
     Approx. 165 Km)
       Wake up to the fresh mountain air and enjoy breakfast before checking out from Sangla.
       Board your vehicle to visit Chitkul, the last inhabited village near the Indo-Tibet border (subject
       to weather and route conditions).
       Take a walk through its traditional wooden houses and soak in views of the Baspa River and
       snow-capped peaks.
       Later, reach Khab Sangam, where the Spiti and Sutlej rivers meet amidst dramatic mountain
       landscapes.
       Continue towards Nako, passing through rugged Himalayan terrain and picturesque villages
       along the way.
       Arrive in Nako by evening and settle into your stay.
       Dinner and overnight stay in Nako.




     DAY 3
     Nako To Tabo Via Lepcha La (Distance: Approx. 120 Km)
       Wake up in Nako and enjoy breakfast amid the high-altitude landscapes of Kinnaur.
       Before departure, spend some time at the serene Nako Lake, surrounded by willow trees and
       mountain views.
       Begin your Spiti Valley backpacking trip towards Tabo, through Spiti's rugged mountain terrain.
       Pass through Lepcha, the o cial gateway to Spiti Valley, known for its surreal canyon views
       and winding roads.
       Later, take a detour to Gue Village, home to the naturally preserved 500-year-old mummy of a
       Buddhist monk (depending on weather and road conditions).
       Enjoy spectacular views of barren mountains, deep gorges, and the Spiti River valley along the
       route.
       Arrive in Tabo by afternoon and settle into your hostel.
       Overnight stay in Tabo.




     DAY 4
     Tabo To Kaza | Tabo, Dhankar, and Key Monastery & Chicham
     Bridge (Distance: Approx. 110 Km)
       After breakfast, visit the ancient Tabo Monastery, over 1,000 years old and often called the
       "Ajanta of the Himalayas."
       Then. we’ll continue to Dhankar Monastery, dramatically perched on a cliff.
       Enjoy panoramic views of the Spiti and Pin River valleys from this historic former capital of
       Spiti.
       Later, head towards Key Monastery, the largest and most iconic monastery in Spiti Valley.
       Spend some time exploring its prayer halls, murals, and hilltop setting overlooking the valley
       below.
       Later, we will stop at Chicham Bridge, one of Asia's highest suspension bridges spanning a
       deep mountain gorge.
       Arrive in Kaza, check in to your hostel, and relax after a day of exploration.




     DAY 5
     Kaza Sightseeing | Hikkim, Komic, Langza & Kaza Market
     (Distance: Approx. 50 km)
       After breakfast, set out to explore some of the world's highest inhabited villages around Kaza.
       Visit Hikkim, home to the world's highest post o ce, and send a postcard from this remote
       Himalayan village.
       Continue to Komic, one of the highest motorable villages in the world, known for its
       breathtaking mountain setting.
       Later, head to Langza, famous for its giant Buddha statue and breathtaking views of Chau
       Chau Kang Nilda peak.
       Take a walk through the village and keep an eye out for marine fossils dating back millions of
       years.
       Return to Kaza by evening and spend some time exploring the local market, cafés, and
       souvenir shops.




     DAY 6
     Kaza To Kalpa Via Reckong Peo & Suicide Point (Distance:
     Approx. 205 Km)
       After breakfast, check out and continue your Spiti Valley backpacking tour towards Kalpa.
       Enjoy a scenic drive through deep gorges, river valleys, and some of the most stunning
       landscapes in the Himalayas.
       Upon arrival, visit Reckong Peo, the commercial hub of Kinnaur, known for its local markets
       and views of the Kinner Kailash range.
       Later, stop at the famous Suicide Point, a narrow cliffside stretch offering breathtaking views
       of the surrounding valleys and mountains.
       Reach Kalpa by evening and check in to your hostel.
       If the weather is clear, enjoy stunning sunset views over the snow-capped Kinner Kailash
       peaks.
       Overnight stay in Kalpa.




     DAY 7
     Kalpa To Shimla (Distance: Approx. 210 Km) | Overnight Journey
     To Delhi
       Enjoy your breakfast and check out of your hostel.
       Board your transfer towards Shimla, passing pine forests, river valleys, and charming hillside
       towns.
       As you arrive in Shimla and enjoy some time to freshen up before your departure.
       Later, board your overnight Volvo to Delhi, bringing your Spiti backpacking trip to a memorable
       close.
